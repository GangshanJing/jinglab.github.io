Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning has become the most popular tool for cooperative decision making in dynamic environments. As there are so many relevant works emerging everyday,  we put our main focus on the following two aspects:

**Graph-Based Scalability**

The scalability issue mainly comes from the complex couplings between different agents. We believe that modeling these couplings by graphs can help to decompose the whole problem. Along this direction, we have achieved hierarchical learning for balancing the optimality and scalability {%cite jingModelFreeOptimalControl2021%}, asynchornous learning {%cite jingAsynchronousDistributedReinforcement2024%}, and graph-induced local learning for high scalability {%cite jingLearningDistributedStabilizing2022%}.

**Graph-Based Optimality**

Optimality is an important topic for decision making problems. However, evaluating the optimality of a policy generated by MARL has always been difficult. By modeling the inter-agent couplings based on graphs, we want to establish the relationship between optimality and the graph structure. In our recent work {%cite ding2025action%}, we provided the graphical condition on the action-dependency graph for global optimality in the context of coordinated RL.
