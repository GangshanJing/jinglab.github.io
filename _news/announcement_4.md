Our paper “<a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10463092">Distributed Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Based on Graph-Induced Local Value Functions</a>” has been accepted by IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control as a full paper. This is a joint work with He Bai at Oklahoma State University, Jemin George at US ARL, Aranya Chakrabortty at North Carolina State University, and Piyush K. Sharma at US ARL.
