Our paper "On the Equivalence between Signed Angle Rigidity and Bearing Rigidity" has been accepted by IEEE CDC 2025. This paper establishes the complete relationship between the two rigidity theories for the first time, and significantly relaxes the condition on the sensing graph for network localization and formation control via signed angles. The full version has been put in arXiv. [Click here to check it!](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19945)
